# Side Button Ramping Mode Operation Instructions

## Standard Driver — Excluding LHP73B

### Basic Operation

1. **Turn On / Ramping**
   - Click the button to turn the flashlight on at the **last memorized brightness**.
   - Press and hold to **ramp brightness up**.
   - Release, then press and hold again to **ramp brightness down**.

2. **Moonlight**
   - From **Off**, press and hold the button.
   - Activates **Moonlight Mode (0.2%)**.
   - Moonlight brightness is **not memorized**.

3. **Turbo**
   - From **On or Off**, double-click the button.
   - Activates **Turbo (100%)**.

### Special Modes

4. **Strobe**
   - From **Off**, triple-click the button.
   - Activates **Strobe Mode**.

5. **Tactical Mode**
   - From **Off**, quadruple-click the button.
   - Tactical Mode operates at **100% brightness only**.
   - Quadruple-click again to exit Tactical Mode.

6. **Voltage Check**
   - From **Off**, click the button five times.
   - The flashlight reports battery voltage using flashes.
   - Example: `*** pause *******` indicates **3.7 V**.

7. **Stepped Mode**
   - From **Off**, click the button six times.
   - Switches between smooth ramping and four stepped brightness levels:
     - **1%**
     - **10%**
     - **40%**
     - **100%**

8. **Lockout Mode**
   - From **Off**, click the button ten times.
   - The flashlight will blink once in response to any button operation while locked.
   - Click ten times again to exit Lockout Mode.
   - **Ten clicks is the only way to unlock the flashlight.**

### Protection Features

The flashlight includes:

- **Low-voltage protection**
- **Temperature control protection**

# LHP73B Mode Operation Instructions

## Basic Operation

1. **Turn On / Ramping**
   - Click the button to turn the flashlight on at the **last memorized brightness**.
   - Press and hold to **ramp brightness up**.
   - Release, then press and hold again to **ramp brightness down**.

2. **Moonlight**
   - From **Off**, press and hold the button.
   - Activates **Moonlight Mode**.
   - Moonlight brightness is **not memorized**.

3. **10 A Turbo**
   - From **On or Off**, double-click the button.
   - Activates the **10 A brightness level**.
   - This brightness level is **not memorized**.

4. **20 A Momentary Turbo**
   - From **On or Off**, double-click and hold the button.
   - Activates the **20 A brightness level** while the button is held.
   - When released, the flashlight returns to the previous brightness level or turns off if activated from Off.

### Special Modes

5. **Strobe**
   - From **Off**, triple-click the button.
   - Activates **Strobe Mode**.

6. **Tactical Mode**
   - From **Off**, quadruple-click the button.
   - Tactical Mode operates at **20 A**.
   - Quadruple-click again to exit Tactical Mode.

7. **Voltage Check**
   - From **Off**, click the button five times.
   - The flashlight reports battery voltage using flashes.
   - Example: `*** pause *******` indicates **3.7 V**.

8. **Stepped Mode**
   - From **Off**, click the button six times.
   - Switches from smooth ramping to **four stepped brightness levels**.
   - Maximum current in stepped mode is **10 A**.

9. **Lockout Mode**
   - From **Off**, click the button ten times.
   - The flashlight will blink once in response to any button operation while locked.
   - Click ten times again to exit Lockout Mode.
   - **Ten clicks is the only way to unlock the flashlight.**

### Protection Features

The flashlight includes:

- **Low-voltage protection**
- **Temperature control protection**
