# FireflyLite Inventory

## What Kind of Lights Does a Light Maker Use When He Doesn't Use His Own?

**These crazy bastards.**

TFU lights are deliberately simple, durable tools: efficient regulated drivers, useful modes, replaceable cells, good emitters, and as little unnecessary bullshit as possible.

FireflyLite is what happens when I want something else.

These are enthusiast lights: Andúril 2, excellent regulated drivers, RGB auxiliary LEDs, unusual high-CRI emitters, interchangeable optics, magnetic tails, integrated charging, and sometimes features whose primary justification is **because it's fucking cool**.

This is the working inventory, configuration record, use-case map, and wish list.

## Design Features I Actually Value

The recurring features explain why these lights coexist with TFU builds rather than replacing them.

**Efficient regulated drivers.** High output is useful; sustained predictable output is better.

**High-CRI emitters with intentional tint.** Neutral 5000K is useful. Rosy 3500–4000K is beautiful. Both have jobs.

**Optical modularity.** The X1S demonstrates why this matters particularly well: changing an optic can turn essentially the same electrical system into a smooth field light, narrow TIR light, or conventional reflector thrower.

**Continuous ramping.** For field and task use, choosing exactly as much light as necessary is genuinely useful. Walking illumination and “light up that tree” do not need to be predetermined modes.

**Standard cylindrical cells.** 18650 and 21700 remain replaceable, externally chargeable, readily available energy storage.

**USB-C where appropriate.** Particularly valuable on larger field/general-purpose lights and anything with powerbank capability.

**Magnetic tails.** Sometimes annoying near phones. Extremely useful everywhere else.

**Andúril 2.** Excessively complicated until configured correctly; excellent afterward.

**Serviceability and accessories.** Replaceable optics, spare O-rings, interchangeable parts, and labeled hard cases mean the light is treated as equipment rather than a sealed consumer appliance.

## The Point

TFU answers:

> **What flashlight would I build to be simple, durable, repairable, and useful?**

These FireflyLite lights answer:

> **What happens when flashlight people are allowed to get weird?**

Apparently the answer is efficient boost drivers, CRI95 emitters, swappable optics, RGB status lights, magnetic tails, Andúril, extremely good machining, and an alarming quantity of rosy photons.

**These crazy bastards.**
